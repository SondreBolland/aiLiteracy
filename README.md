# AI literacy for first-semester university students
This repository contains material for a lecture providing freshers information on GenAI and the effects of its uses. For this we target AI literacy, instructing students in:
* What is AI?
* What can AI do?
* How does AI work?
* How should AI be used?
* How do people perceive AI?

## Materials
1. An (uncompleted) document describing AI literacy and information for first-semester students formed on its basis
2. A video presentation of this information
